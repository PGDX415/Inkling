拾光（Inkling）隐私政策

拾光是一款极简日记应用。我们深知隐私对您的重要性，因此从设计之初便将隐私保护作为核心原则。本隐私政策将清晰说明我们如何处理您的数据。

1. 数据存储与同步

您的所有日记内容、照片和个人资料均存储在您的设备本地，并通过您个人的 iCloud 账户（CloudKit）在您的设备间同步。拾光不会将您的数据上传至任何第三方服务器，也不会在任何情况下访问您的日记内容。iCloud 同步完全由 Apple 提供，受 Apple 隐私政策保护。

2. AI 润色功能

拾光提供可选的 AI 润色功能，支持 DeepSeek、硅基流动和 Google Gemini 三种服务商。当您使用 AI 润色时，您选中的日记文本将被发送至对应服务商的 API 进行处理。此功能完全由您主动触发，拾光不会在后台自动发送任何内容。各 AI 服务商的数据处理政策请参阅其各自的隐私政策。

3. 生物识别认证

拾光支持使用面容 ID 或触控 ID 锁定应用。此功能完全在设备本地完成，您的生物识别数据由 Apple 操作系统管理，拾光无法访问您的面容或指纹数据。

4. 数据导出与删除

您可以随时导出所有日记为文本文件，也可以随时删除单篇日记或清空所有数据。删除操作不可撤销，数据将从您的设备和 iCloud 中永久移除。

5. 第三方服务

除您主动选择的 AI 润色服务外，拾光不使用任何第三方分析工具、广告 SDK 或数据追踪服务。我们不收集任何使用统计或崩溃报告。

6. 儿童隐私

拾光不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 与我们联系。

Inkling Privacy Policy

Inkling is a minimalist journaling app. We take your privacy seriously and have built it with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Storage & Sync

All your journal entries, photos, and profile data are stored locally on your device and synced across your devices via your personal iCloud account (CloudKit). Inkling does not upload your data to any third-party servers, nor do we ever access your journal content. iCloud sync is provided entirely by Apple and protected by Apple's privacy policy.

2. AI Polish Feature

Inkling offers an optional AI polish feature powered by DeepSeek, SiliconFlow, and Google Gemini. When you use AI polish, the selected journal text is sent to the chosen provider's API for processing. This feature is triggered only by your explicit action. Inkling never sends any content in the background. Please refer to each AI provider's respective privacy policy for their data handling practices.

3. Biometric Authentication

Inkling supports app locking via Face ID or Touch ID. This functionality is handled entirely on-device. Your biometric data is managed by the Apple operating system — Inkling cannot and does not access your face or fingerprint data.

4. Data Export & Deletion

You can export all your entries as a text file at any time, and you can delete individual entries or clear all data. Deletion is irreversible — data will be permanently removed from your device and iCloud.

5. Third-Party Services

Beyond the AI polish services you actively choose to use, Inkling does not integrate any third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or data tracking services. We do not collect usage statistics or crash reports.

6. Children's Privacy

Inkling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues.
